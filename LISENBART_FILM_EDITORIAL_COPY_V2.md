## 1. The commercial job

**Business goals:** (1) win animation / creative production commissions; (2) develop partnerships around original films and IP; (3) demonstrate professional credibility through factual evidence.

**Audience priorities:** Agency producers and creative leads; brand production/marketing managers; other production studios; film producers, distributors and IP partners; game advertisers and AI-production clients routed to specialist offerings.

**Brand promise / editorial spine:** **Creative thinking. Directorial judgement. Production discipline.** This is an internal editing standard, not a new public slogan. The established public brand line remains **“Endless stories. Different worlds. One language.”**

**Voice:** confident but not boastful; short, specific, contemporary English; collaborative rather than self-congratulatory; avoid promises of guaranteed speed, savings, capacity or performance; say what can be commissioned and what the next step is. The studio speaks as “we”; the private biography speaks as “I”.

**Global editorial rules:**
- One primary task per page; no repeated About/Selected Clients/reels across pages merely for decoration.
- Refer to **LISENBART FILM** as the studio and its commissioning business, **Originals** as owned creative projects, **GAMES** as the established specialist production offering, and **AI** as a capability (no GLOWL link yet).
- Avoid suggesting a fixed team headcount, owned global offices, guaranteed turnaround, universal project manager model, legal rights availability, or awards not documented.
- Never conflate company age (since 2006) with founder career (since 1991).
- Names, metrics and rights statuses need verification where marked below.
